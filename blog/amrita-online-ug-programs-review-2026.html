Amrita Online UG Programs 2026 — Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham offers three online UG degree programmes through Amrita Online: BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications), BCom (Bachelor of Commerce), and BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration). All are from NAAC A++ accredited Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham — India's highest accreditation grade. WES recognised. Industry certifications bundled at no extra cost.

Amrita BCA vs Amrita BCom vs Amrita BBA — Which to Choose?

Choose Amrita BCA if: you want a computer science foundation for software development, data science, or IT career. Your interest is in programming, databases, and computing.

Choose Amrita BCom if: you want a commerce foundation for accounting, finance, CA Foundation pursuit, or BFSI career. Your interest is in accounts, economics, and financial markets.

Choose Amrita BBA if: you want a business management foundation for marketing, HR, operations, or management trainee roles. Your interest is in business strategy, management, and general commerce.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amrita online UG degree valid in India?

Yes — all Amrita online UG degrees (BCA, BCom, BBA) are UGC-DEB approved from NAAC A++ accredited Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. They are valid for employment, higher studies, and government job eligibility in India. WES recognised for international use.

Which Amrita online UG program has best placement?

Amrita Online BCA has the strongest placement support — Oracle and Amazon certifications bundled make graduates more competitive for IT sector roles. BBA has growing placement support for management trainee roles. Across all three, placement outcomes depend heavily on the student's self-initiative, certifications, and project portfolio.

What is the eligibility for Amrita Online BCA/BCom/BBA?

Eligibility for all three: Class 12 pass with minimum 50% marks from any recognised board. No stream restriction — Arts, Commerce, and Science students are all eligible. No entrance examination required for online programmes.

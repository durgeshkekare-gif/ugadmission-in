CUET UG 2026 — Why It Matters More Than Boards Now

CUET (Common University Entrance Test) UG 2026 is the primary admission route for central universities including Delhi University (DU), Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Jamia Millia Islamia, and 200+ other universities. With 4.5+ lakh seats across universities using CUET scores, scoring well in CUET has largely replaced board marks for central university admissions. A strong CUET score can compensate for lower board percentage at most participating universities.

Subject-Wise Preparation Tips

Commerce Students (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics)

  • Accountancy: NCERT Class 12 is the primary resource — all concepts and formats
  • Business Studies: theory heavy — practice writing key definitions and case studies
  • Economics: quantitative economics + theory — practice diagrams and numerical problems
  • General Test: logical reasoning and data interpretation are high-score areas for commerce students

Science Students (Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Maths)

  • NCERT Class 11+12 are non-negotiable primary resources for all science subjects
  • Physics + Chemistry: formula application and conceptual MCQs — timed practice essential
  • Biology: NCERT diagrams and definitions — high memory component
  • General Test: quants section is a strength area — leverage it for high scores

Arts Students (History, Political Science, Sociology, Geography)

  • All arts subjects: NCERT Class 12 + important terms and timeline memorisation
  • History: chronological events, important treaties, cultural aspects — frequent CUET topics
  • Political Science: concepts, constitutional articles, recent developments
  • General Test: current affairs and GK are strong areas for arts students

Best Resources for CUET UG 2026

  • NCERTs Class 11+12 (primary) — all domain subjects
  • NTA CUET previous year papers 2022, 2023, 2024 (essential)
  • Arihant CUET domain subject books (good for MCQ practice)
  • Career Power, Oswaal, and Disha CUET mock test series
  • YouTube channels: Unacademy CUET, Physics Wallah CUET for free conceptual videos

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CUET UG 2026 exam date?

NTA has not announced the official CUET UG 2026 dates as of this writing. Based on previous years, CUET UG is typically conducted in May-June. Applications usually open in February-March. Register on the NTA CUET official website (cuet.samarth.ac.in) and check for official notification.

How many attempts are allowed in CUET UG?

There is no attempt limit for CUET UG — you can appear every year. The best score from your attempt is used for that year's admission cycle. Unlike JEE or NEET, CUET has no restriction on the number of attempts over your academic career.

Is CUET harder than JEE and NEET?

CUET is significantly easier than JEE Advanced and NEET for science subjects. CUET is based entirely on NCERT Class 11-12 syllabus at standard difficulty. JEE Advanced and NEET test at higher conceptual levels with more complex problem-solving. However, CUET's competitive aspect comes from high attempt rates and section selection strategy, not raw difficulty.
